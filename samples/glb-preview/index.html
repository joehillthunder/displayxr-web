URL parameters: ?glb=<url> one asset · ?list=<manifest.json> a list ([{name, url, size_m, kit, licence}], or {assets: […]}; URLs relative to the manifest) · &licence= a licence line · &depth=0–200 start depth in % of comfort. Drop a .glb on the tile to open it. Files are read with cache: 'no-store' and never stored by this page. A <name>.stability.json sidecar next to a GLB (written by displayxr-stability) is shown as its AI-generation provenance.
